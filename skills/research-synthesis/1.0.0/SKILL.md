# Research synthesis

Build a source inventory with title, date and provenance. Distinguish direct observations, participant claims, analyst interpretation and unknowns. Use only accessible source contents; a filename or abstract is not evidence for unseen details.

Group observations by recurring question or behavior. Track contradictory evidence and sample limitations. A repeated statement from one participant does not count as agreement across participants. Support each material finding with source references that the user can inspect.

For a decision memo, lead with the decision it supports, then evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs and unresolved questions. For a thematic summary, retain minority or negative observations that could affect the conclusion. Do not manufacture quotes, sample sizes or statistical confidence.

Keep personal information out of the report unless the user's purpose requires it. End with what evidence would change the conclusions.
