# Product brief

Extract the intended user, triggering situation, desired outcome and stated constraints. Preserve explicit scope, business model and user preferences across revisions. Mark inferred choices as assumptions rather than accepted requirements.

Describe the normal path with observable inputs, actions and outputs. Include consequential empty, error, cancellation and recovery states. Define acceptance criteria in terms of behavior that can be tested, not vague adjectives such as complete or intelligent.

Connect each requirement to a user need. Distinguish product behavior from implementation choices. Identify dependencies that genuinely need external accounts, credentials, content or business decisions, while completing the portions supported by existing information.

Deliver a coherent brief with scenarios, requirements, data boundaries and acceptance criteria. Do not introduce subscriptions, user accounts or new distribution channels unless the user's model requires them.
